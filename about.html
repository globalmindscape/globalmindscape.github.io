About

Commercial experience. Structured thinking. Practical systems.

Global Mindscape helps businesses understand what is affecting their commercial performance, and put practical improvements in place.

Our philosophy

Diagnose first. Then treat.

Sales performance depends on more than individual effort. It depends on how a business finds prospects, manages enquiries, follows up opportunities, converts customers, keeps accounts and learns from its numbers.

Our approach starts with a structured diagnosis. We examine how these connected activities work in practice, identify weaknesses and set priorities before recommending what to change. Where a business needs more capacity, we can also provide hands-on support.

The aim is practical work that fits the people and systems already in the business.

Founder and Director

Philip Annamalai

Philip has more than 20 years of B2B sales management experience, including substantial regional sales management. His work has covered revenue growth, territory management, sales teams, market share, competitive environments, account management and distribution networks.

He supports that experience with structured learning in sales, data and behaviour, and brings both to diagnosing sales challenges and developing practical solutions.

Business systems

From findings to working systems.

Alongside commercial process improvement, Philip designs business workflows and directs the development of practical digital tools using AI-assisted development. This includes business data, reporting, workflow automation and application interfaces.

His role is to define what the business needs, direct the build and carry out user acceptance testing. It is presented as business systems design and AI-assisted application development, not as professional software engineering.
