Why we give you a Pipeline Tracker

Some businesses have no proper way of knowing where their own deals are. Not because they are careless, but because nobody ever gave them a simple way to keep track. The business is run from someone's head, a WhatsApp thread or a spreadsheet nobody trusts.

The Audit looks at exactly this in Lead Management and in Reporting & Analytics. So we give you a tool to help close the gap, free to every Sales Growth Audit client, with no software fee on top of our service fee.

What the audit looks at

Five areas of your sales process

Each area is looked at on its own, so you can see where the weaknesses are.

Lead generation

How you create awareness and find suitable prospects.

Lead management

What happens once a lead arrives: recording, qualifying and following up.

Sales conversion

How well real opportunities are moved to a buying decision.

Customer retention

How you keep customers and earn repeat business.

Reporting and analytics

Whether you have the numbers to see what is happening and decide what to do.

A mathematical model sits behind the audit and turns your answers into an estimated rand figure for sales leakage. The questions and scoring stay private, so every business is assessed the same way.

Our diagnoses and recommended fixes are research-informed. The Sales Growth Audit draws on established sales research, recognised commercial frameworks and behavioural science. Our approach references published work by researchers and authors including Neil Rackham (SPIN Selling), Matthew Dixon and Brent Adamson (The Challenger Sale), Robert Kaplan and David Norton (the Balanced Scorecard) and Robert Cialdini (Influence), alongside relevant research published in Harvard Business Review.

We do not copy global findings. We study them and adapt them to South African businesses and to each business's own circumstances. The scoring and weights behind the Audit are our own.
