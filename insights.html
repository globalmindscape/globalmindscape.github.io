Insights

Practical thinking for better sales execution.

Short, useful perspectives on the systems, habits and decisions that shape commercial performance in small and medium-sized businesses.

Sales management

Is your sales pipeline a record or a management tool?

A pipeline is useful only when it reflects real opportunities and supports a clear next action. A list of names and values cannot, on its own, tell a business what needs attention. Effective pipeline discipline asks: what is the current stage, what has happened, what must happen next, who owns the action and when is it due?

Lead management

Enquiries need a process, not good intentions.

When enquiries arrive through calls, email and messaging apps, follow-up can become dependent on memory. A simple process for recording, assigning and reviewing enquiries makes it easier to see what has been handled and what still needs attention.

Commercial reporting

Start with numbers that help you make a decision.

Reporting should answer practical management questions. Which opportunities are progressing? Where are deals stalling? How long are quotations taking? What proportion of opportunities are won? A small set of consistently defined measures is more useful than a large collection of numbers nobody acts on.
