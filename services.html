Phase 1 · Diagnosis

Sales Growth Audit

R7,500 once-off

A structured assessment of the business's sales process, designed to identify weaknesses and help establish where corrective action should begin.

Two assessment versions

  • Field Sales: for businesses whose sales activity depends on territory coverage, customer visits and direct field execution.
  • Quotes & Proposals: for businesses that generate enquiries and convert them through quotations, proposals or tenders.

What the Audit includes

  • 15 diagnostic questions across five pillars, plus four questions about business numbers.
  • A structured report with findings and estimated sales leakage based on the information supplied.
  • Red, amber and green indicators to help interpret the diagnostic results.
  • Research-informed recommendations adapted to the business's circumstances.
  • A 60-minute online review with Philip.
  • Sales Calculator, Pipeline Tracker and relevant resources for the five diagnostic pillars, included as additional client benefits.

The Audit is a diagnostic assessment. Estimates depend on the accuracy and completeness of information supplied. It does not guarantee a particular increase in revenue or profit.
