Sales Growth Audit

R7,500 once-off
Discuss the Sales Growth Audit

The Sales Growth Audit examines how your sales operation works, identifies weaknesses and opportunities, and provides practical recommendations. It covers five connected pillars: Lead Generation, Lead Management, Sales Conversion, Customer Retention, and Reporting & Analytics.

Two assessment approaches

  • Field Sales for businesses that rely on field representatives, territories, account visits, distribution and direct field execution.
  • Quote & Proposal for businesses that generate enquiries, qualify opportunities, prepare quotations, proposals or tenders, and manage a pipeline through to conversion.

Businesses that use both sales motions can be assessed using both approaches.

What the Audit includes

  • An in-depth diagnostic review of the sales operation across five connected pillars, supported by an assessment of relevant business figures.
  • A report presenting the findings, with red, amber and green indicators.
  • Research-informed diagnoses and recommendations, with practical fixes adapted to your business.
  • A 60-minute online review of the findings and priorities.
  • A Sales Calculator, a Pipeline Tracker and the five-pillar resources that match your findings.

Research-informed, with our own scoring

We study established research and recognised commercial frameworks, then adapt the relevant principles to your circumstances. These include Neil Rackham’s SPIN Selling, Matthew Dixon and Brent Adamson’s The Challenger Sale, Robert Kaplan and David Norton’s Balanced Scorecard, Robert Cialdini’s Influence and research published in Harvard Business Review.

The scoring and weighting behind the Audit are Global Mindscape’s own.

Any estimate of possible sales leakage depends on the accuracy and completeness of the information you supply. The Audit does not guarantee a particular increase in revenue or profit.

Ongoing support

Fractional Business Development & Sales Leadership

From R5,500 per month
Discuss Fractional support

Strategic business development and implementation support for businesses that need extra commercial capacity or an independent perspective. It complements an existing sales manager and does not have to replace one.

Areas of support can include

  • Business development planning and opportunity identification.
  • Pipeline reviews, qualification and prioritisation.
  • Follow-up and sales progression.
  • Sales-team routines and accountability.
  • Territory and account development.
  • Conversion improvement, customer retention and account growth.
  • Commercial reporting and visibility.
  • Sales-process documentation and improvement.
